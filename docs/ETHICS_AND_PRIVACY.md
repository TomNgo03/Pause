# Ethics and Privacy Specification

## Purpose boundary

Pause supports reflection about digital habits. It is not a diagnostic, therapeutic, parental-surveillance, or academic-monitoring product.

## Required protections

- Participation and Screen Time authorization are voluntary.
- Prototype Mode remains available when permission is declined.
- Reflections may be skipped.
- Overrides are not labeled as failures.
- Data is local by default.
- No third-party advertising or behavioral analytics.
- No collection of messages, content, URLs, contacts, location, grades, or health diagnoses.
- No public leaderboard or individual comparison.
- Delete-all-data is accessible from Settings.
- Study exports use participant codes and relative study days.

## Minor participants

Before a school-associated pilot, obtain the school/mentor's approval and determine applicable guardian-consent requirements. Explain purpose, duration, collected fields, risks, withdrawal, deletion, and contact information in plain language. Do not pressure classmates to participate.

## Language rules

Use: intentional, automatic, planned, unplanned, reflected, skipped.

Avoid: addicted, dopamine detox, failure, lazy, disciplined, cure, treatment, improves grades, improves mental health.

## Retention

The participant controls on-device retention. The project team should delete exported pilot data after the stated analysis period. Publish aggregates only.
