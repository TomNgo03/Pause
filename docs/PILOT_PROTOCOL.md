# Exploratory Pilot Protocol

## Question

Is Pause understandable, acceptable, technically reliable, and promising enough for longer evaluation?

## Participants and duration

- 8–12 voluntary high-school participants
- 5–7 days of use after a brief onboarding
- Guardian and school approval where required

## Measures

- Sessions planned
- Reflections completed
- Self-reported intention completion: yes, partly, no, skipped
- Perceived control: in control, neutral, distracted, skipped
- Extension frequency
- Technical failures
- Would-use-again response
- Short exit interview themes

## Exclusions

Do not collect grades, diagnoses, message content, search history, exact URLs, or public rankings. Do not claim causal or medical effects.

## Reporting template

Report participant retention, total sessions, medians or percentages, repeated feedback themes, technical limitations, and design changes. Clearly label results as self-reported and exploratory.
